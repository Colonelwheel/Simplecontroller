FLOATING RINGS AND ACTIVATION ZONES: PLAIN-ENGLISH OPTIONS GUIDE

This describes the current working Simplecontroller implementation. Quoted names match the editor.
The combinations at the bottom are examples to try, not a report of your saved phone settings or a
promise about a particular game's sensitivity.

1. WHAT A FLOATING RING DOES

An activation zone is an invisible rectangle in Play. A fresh touch in otherwise unused space
inside it brings up its ring and starts the saved starting function under your finger. The
rectangle decides WHERE you can summon the ring; it does not determine the ring's size.

Movement sends a virtual controller stick. Aim sends mouse movement or the right controller stick.
Boost 1 and Boost 2 are extra actions reached at distances from the ring's center. An Aim knob
selects an aiming/firing setup. ADS means aiming down sights; the app only sends your chosen
binding, and the game decides what it does.

A "payload" is a binding such as LT:1.0, RT:1.0, X360A or W. LT and RT are the virtual controller
triggers; 1.0 means fully pressed. A comma-separated binding can hold several outputs together,
such as W,SHIFT. It is not a timed sequence. The ring's Tap/Hold/Repeat controls supply timing; do
not put a separate timed trigger pulse inside the binding.

Ring distances use dp: Android size units adjusted for screen density. Activation rectangles and
some Right Stick tuning use px: actual screen pixels. They are different units. Timing uses ms:
1000 ms is one second; 300 ms is 0.3 second. X/Y movement values of -1 to 1 are proportions, not
distances.

Floating controls need the compatible Windows receiver's acknowledgement. If connection
preparation is unfinished, a touch can wait up to 1500 ms while the same finger remains down. When
ready, the ring appears at the latest finger position with a fresh timing start. Waiting movement
is not replayed as aiming or Boost travel. Lifting cancels that pending summon.

2. CREATE, POSITION AND SAVE AN ACTIVATION ZONE

In Edit, use + -> Floating ring -> New or Saved Presets. + -> Manage floating controls, or holding
Edit, opens the floating-control manager. It lists covered zones by name, so you can edit one even
when another outline or button covers it.

"Name" identifies the zone and its Finish-list entry. "Activation zone enabled" permits summoning
it in Play. Turning it off keeps its saved configuration.

"Zone left (px)" and "Zone top (px)" position the rectangle. "Zone width (px)" and "Zone height
(px)" resize it. Each has an exact field and slider. Drag an outline in Edit to move it; lift
commits the drag. Cancellation or lost contact rolls the unfinished drag back. Tap or hold an
outline to open settings.

Rectangle changes affect the current orientation. Portrait and Landscape have separate saved
arrangements. Resizing the activation rectangle does not resize the ring, its Aim targets or
grouped buttons. Existing buttons and app controls keep touch priority; a zone behind them cannot
steal their initial touch.

Settings dialogs edit a draft. Save the inner dialog, then Save the floating-zone dialog, then
save the controller profile normally. Canceling the outer dialog discards its draft changes.
Saving a reusable preset is a separate library action, not the same as saving your controller
profile.

"Duplicate floating zone and its members" makes an independent copy. "Move zone above overlapping
zones" changes which dormant zone wins where activation rectangles overlap. It does not outrank
existing visible buttons. "Remove zone" removes a ring zone. For floated buttons/groups, "Ungroup
and restore original geometry" restores their saved original arrangements while keeping their
latest button settings.

The manager also creates a floating Button/group by rectangle selection. "Initial Button (Reveal
only produces no input)" chooses its first button, or Reveal only for a group. "Swipe between
compatible ordinary members" controls movement between its buttons. Their individual settings
remain in the ordinary button editor. The rest of this guide concentrates on rings.

3. DEFAULTS AND HOW A TOUCH KEEPS ITS JOB

A new ring has Movement and Aim enabled, Boosts disabled, and one centered Aim knob. Its inner Aim
radius is 48 dp; Movement radius is 88 dp. It starts as MOVEMENT, with neutral initial X/Y. Aim
uses MOUSE, sensitivity 1 and LINEAR_RELATIVE. Fire mode is NONE; ADS is blank. The stored Fire
payload RT:1.0 does nothing while Fire mode is NONE.

Separate action sets, custom geometry, closer-spacing options, both direct-Aim options, direct
B2-to-B1 return, swipe-resistant Finish, fine aiming, qualified firing and Boost-entry fire timing
all start OFF. Direct taps start OFF. Existing saved rings retain their settings; these defaults
do not reset them.

The first touch in a dormant activation rectangle always uses "Summon starting role". It does not
inspect which invisible band happened to lie under that touch. A direct Boost touch normally means
a new touch on a direct band of an already visible ring. Selecting a saved BOOST starting role can
also summon directly into that action.

Movement stays Movement if you drag inward. Aim stays Aim when you drag outward through Boosts. A
direct Boost touch keeps its captured Boost role even if an optional setting adds Aim later.
Lifting permits a new touch to choose another function. Extra fingers do not take over the
captured touch.

The ring remains visible after lift until Finish or cleanup. Several different zones can have
visible instances. One zone does not create multiple simultaneous copies of itself.

4. RING GEOMETRY, STARTING ROLE AND DIRECT AIM

Open "Ring geometry, layers and starting role".

"Inner aim radius (dp)" sizes the inner Aim background. "Movement outer radius (dp)" sizes
Movement and its full-strength reference. The Movement radius must exceed the positive Aim radius,
even if a layer is disabled. These are distances from the shared center; there are no
center-offset controls.

"Summon starting role" offers MOVEMENT, AIM, BOOST_ONE and BOOST_TWO. MOVEMENT uses the Movement
settings and initial vector. AIM starts "Starting aim knob", which selects the default ordinary
Aim setup for summon and the inner background. Its list shows existing knob identifiers, such as
aim_1. One choice means only one knob exists; use "Aim knobs and firing" -> "Add aim knob" for
more. A BOOST role starts only its selected direct stage and requires that stage and its direct
taps to be enabled. Choose an enabled role explicitly when disabling layers.

"Boost 1 immediately outside Movement" brings Movement's first drag threshold just beyond the
Movement radius. Boost 2 keeps the saved B1-to-B2 gap: default 120/160 dp becomes 88/128 dp. It
does not enable Boosts or Movement participation.

"Compact boosts while aiming" brings an original Aim gesture's B1 just outside inner Aim, with B2
at the Movement radius: defaults 48/88 dp. Enable "Aiming participates in boosts" separately.
During that compact Aim gesture the Movement background/marker is hidden; it returns on lift.
Movement/direct-started gestures keep their original jobs.

With either closer-spacing option enabled, direct-tap bands are automatically placed outside
visible Movement, using the saved starting role's bank and matching band widths. If Movement is
disabled they start outside Aim. This prevents visible base areas from swallowing the direct
bands. With neither closer spacing nor custom geometry, the saved finite direct areas remain in
use. A bank's enabled custom geometry overrides its automatic spacing.

"Start aiming when a direct boost enters inner area" adds Aim when that same Boost finger moves
inward into Aim. It starts a fresh aiming grab there, without accumulated outward travel. It does
not start the destination knob's ordinary ADS/fire actions automatically.

"Start aiming immediately on direct Boost touch" adds Aim on the direct Boost DOWN instead. It
starts neutrally at that contact and immediately uses configured fine sensitivity if fine aiming
is enabled, without requiring a prior qualifying return. It remains independent of the inner-entry
option. If "Continue aiming outside inner boundary" is OFF, outside movement still pauses Aim
until inner return.

"Direct boost aim destination" selects the existing knob used by either direct-Aim option. One
choice here likewise means only one knob exists, not that the option is broken. Aim and that knob
must be enabled. These options add aiming; Boost bindings and explicitly enabled qualified firing
remain separate. They do not invent ADS or ordinary Fire-mode activation.

"Bank when the saved starting role is direct Boost" matters for saved BOOST_ONE/BOOST_TWO starts.
Saved AIM always gives direct taps the Aim bank; saved MOVEMENT always gives them Movement. For
saved Boost starts, this selector chooses Movement or Aim. The bank stays fixed when the Boost
finger later aims.

"Activate Boost 1 when returning from direct Boost 2" allows inward B2 return to start B1. It is
OFF by default. Crossing below B2's direct inner edge unlocks enabled B1 for that gesture; B1
direct taps need not be enabled. B1 follows its normal action, threshold and exit rules. A fast
swipe that skips the band still visits it once, then applies exit/latch behavior. Lift or
cancellation cannot start B1. This does not change B2's delay.

5. MOVEMENT SETTINGS AND THE ORANGE RE-CENTER BUTTON

"Movement enabled" enables this layer. "Movement stick" selects LEFT_STICK or RIGHT_STICK. For
ordinary controller movement plus controller aiming, LEFT_STICK here and RIGHT_STICK for Aim avoid
competing for one stick.

"Movement sensitivity" adjusts output. With "Movement response" LINEAR and sensitivity 1, 22/44/88
dp from center give 25%/50%/100% at an 88 dp radius. Output caps at full strength. Lower linear
sensitivity also lowers maximum output. Boost thresholds still use raw distance, not
sensitivity-adjusted output.

RESPONSE_CURVE changes the two axes separately. Below 1 it softens the center but still reaches
full output at the edge; above 1 it responds more strongly near center. RADIAL_RESPONSE_CURVE
curves distance while preserving direction, which is useful for gentle diagonals. Sensitivity 0
produces no Movement output; supported sensitivity values are 0 through 5.

"Auto-center on lift" ON releases Movement when you lift. OFF retains its last vector, letting
movement continue while your finger operates another control. It does not retain ADS or fire.

The existing orange Re-center button centers surfaced rings' Movement, including retained
movement. It keeps the ring visible and leaves its Aim, ADS, fire and Boost actions alone. It also
retains its existing ordinary-stick/Button Aim recenter duties. If used during a held ring
Movement gesture, the current finger position becomes the new Movement neutral. Boost distances
keep the original ring origin. After more deliberate movement, normal retention can apply again.

"Initial X (-1 to 1)" and "Initial Y (-1 to 1)" set the first Movement vector at summon: X
positive is right, negative left; Y positive down, negative up. Use 0/0 for neutral. A vector's
combined length cannot exceed 1; 1/1 is invalid. Response and sensitivity still apply.

"Movement participates in boosts" lets this captured Movement gesture trigger drag Boosts; it
defaults ON. Enabled Boosts are still required. "Exclude movement from Camera Follow" keeps that
Movement out of Camera Follow's movement-based steering calculation. It does not turn off Movement
or globally disable Camera Follow.

6. AIMING AND FINE SENSITIVITY

Open "Aiming and fine sensitivity". "Inner aiming enabled" enables Aim and its knobs. "Aim output"
selects MOUSE, GAME_MOUSE or RIGHT_STICK. MOUSE sends ordinary mouse movement; GAME_MOUSE uses the
receiver's game-relative mouse path; RIGHT_STICK sends the virtual controller's aiming stick. The
game must support the chosen input.

"Normal aim sensitivity" is the normal gain/response setting, 0 through 5. "Invert vertical aim"
reverses vertical Aim. "Mouse profile" LINEAR_RELATIVE follows finger changes directly;
SMOOTHED_NONLINEAR smooths changes and adds nonlinear scaling. These profiles affect mouse
outputs. "Stick response" LINEAR, RESPONSE_CURVE and RADIAL_RESPONSE_CURVE affect RIGHT_STICK,
with the same curve distinction described for Movement.

For RIGHT_STICK, "Stick full displacement (px)" is travel from the initial aiming grab needed for
full stick, normally 220 px. "Stick deadzone (px)" ignores small travel, normally 8 px. More full
displacement requires more finger travel. These are pixels, not dp.

"Stick uses control-center position" replaces that initial-grab displacement mapping with position
relative to the Aim area's center. Then the Aim area's size supplies the position mapping;
changing full-displacement/deadzone values does not tune that center-based mapping. Ordinary
center-based touches can immediately produce nonzero output when away from center. Direct-Aim
additions still establish their neutral grab.

"Aim auto-center on lift" ON centers RIGHT_STICK on lift; OFF can retain its last aiming vector,
shown as "RS held". Mouse movement has no held aiming vector to keep turning after lift. This is
independent of Movement auto-center and all ADS/fire retention.

"Aim haptics" requests touch vibration feedback. "Continue aiming outside inner boundary" defaults
ON. OFF pauses mouse Aim or centers RIGHT_STICK outside, then resumes on return without
accumulated outside travel. It does not itself stop ADS, fire or a Boost timer.

"Aiming participates in boosts" defaults OFF. Turn it on for an Aim-started outward sweep to
qualify Boosts, fine-return behavior and qualified fire. Direct Boost starts can use their own
stages without this switch.

"Suppress Camera Follow while this mouse aim owns a claim" temporarily prevents automatic camera
steering while this ring owns mouse Aim. It is OFF by default. Its claim can survive lift until
explicit End suppression, Finish or cleanup. It does not change the user's global Camera Follow
preference or another surface's claim.

"Fine aim on return from selected boosts" enables the alternate sensitivity, normally 0.4. "Fine
aim qualifies from Boost 1" and "Fine aim qualifies from Boost 2" choose which visits qualify. A
qualifying same-finger return to inner Aim applies "Fine aim sensitivity" immediately. Fine aiming
keeps the selected backend/profile; 0.4 with a curve is not simply 40% maximum output.

"Keep fine sensitivity while qualifying ADS is latched" keeps qualifying fine Aim available while
this ring still owns the associated latched Boost hold, typically ADS. Enable that Boost's Hold
latch and lift retention if it should survive lift. Releasing/replacing that claim ends its fine
qualification. Without that lifetime, fine is gesture-scoped. Immediate direct Aim has the
separate immediate-fine behavior described above.

7. AIM KNOBS, ADS AND FIRE MODES

"Aim knobs and firing" configures up to three knobs; "Add aim knob" creates another. "Knob name"
labels it; "Knob enabled" makes it selectable. "Knob X (inner-radius units)" and "Knob Y
(inner-radius units)" position its center relative to Aim: 0/0 is centered, 0.5 is halfway to that
radius. "Knob target diameter (dp)" sizes its actual touch target. The whole target must fit
inside Aim. At least one knob must remain enabled while Aim is enabled.

Touching a knob selects its firing setup for that touch. The rest of the inner background uses the
saved starting knob. Targets do not turn all surrounding space into nearest-knob regions. A fresh
touch selects another knob; dragging a captured gesture does not switch knobs.

"ADS payload (blank for none)" is independent of "Fire payload (blank for none)". For an ordinary
Aim touch, ADS is held from touch-down unless blank. Fire follows "Fire mode":

NONE: no fire output. Blank ADS as well gives pure aiming. HOLD: keep Fire pressed while eligible.
TAP: one finite press on activation, followed by its release gap. RELEASE_SHOT: arm one shot and
send it on an eligible lift. REPEAT: repeatedly press/release while eligible.

"Fire press duration (ms)" and "Fire release gap (ms)" set Tap/Repeat/Release-shot pulse timing;
defaults are 35/35 ms. Repeat's full cycle is press plus gap. Hold does not repeatedly pulse. The
firing action has at most one pending finite shot rather than an unlimited queue.

"Retain ADS on lift" keeps ordinary ADS held after lift. "Retain fire / repeat on lift" keeps
active HOLD/REPEAT firing. Both default OFF. They do not make an unexpired delay survive lift or
turn TAP/RELEASE_SHOT into indefinite firing.

"Continue ADS outside aim area" and "Continue fire outside aim area" default ON. OFF stops the
corresponding ordinary held/repeating action on Aim exit; it can resume on eligible return. These
do not pause aiming itself. A finite Tap already started completes its transaction; they are not
substitutes for canceling a Release-shot.

"Cancel Release-shot on aim boundary exit" cancels the armed shot when you leave Aim. "Re-arm
Release-shot on return" can restore eligibility on return. Both default OFF. Without boundary
cancellation an eligible release-shot can fire when lifted outside. Cancellation, Finish and hard
cleanup never count as a firing lift.

8. QUALIFIED FIRE AND THE TWO DIFFERENT DELAYS

"Enable qualified boost firing" is OFF by default. ON makes the knob wait for a qualifying Boost
visit instead of immediately activating its normal Fire mode. "Firing qualifies from Boost 1" and
"Firing qualifies from Boost 2" select qualifying stages. "Firing delay (ms)" normally begins when
that same finger returns to Aim. Aiming can resume immediately while firing waits.

"Start fire delay when entering Boost" moves the start to the qualifying Boost entry. It requires
qualified firing to be enabled. With a 300 ms delay and an Aim return after 200 ms, 100 ms
remains. Another qualifying stage or that return does not restart the pending countdown.

With "Continue fire outside aim area" ON, that countdown can expire and fire before Aim return.
With it OFF, an expired countdown becomes ready and activates on Aim return without a second
delay. RELEASE_SHOT only arms at expiry and still needs an eligible later lift. NONE never fires.
A qualifying Boost can have blank bindings: the visit can qualify firing without also sending a
Boost output.

"Cancel pending firing delay when leaving inner area" defaults ON. For normal return-timed fire,
another exit cancels the waiting delay; a new qualified return starts a full delay. For
entry-timed fire, it does not cancel the first outward countdown before the first Aim return.
After that return, a later Aim exit can cancel it. OFF preserves the original pending deadline
across exit; it does not enable outside aiming or override the fire-outside/Release-shot rules.

Lifting before activation cancels pending work. Stop fire, Finish, cancellation and cleanup also
invalidate it. Direct Boosts can use the destination knob's entry-timed qualified firing when
either direct-Aim option is enabled; command-only direct Boosts cannot. This timer does not
automatically start ADS or Aim before the configured direct-Aim behavior does.

"Boost 2 activation delay (ms)" is a separate timer belonging to B2's own binding. Do not assign
delayed RT to both this and the knob's qualified firing for the same action. Their exact controls
and exit behavior are below.

9. SHARED ACTIONS AND THE TWO BANKS

A bank means one stored pair of Boost action settings. "Use separate Movement / Aim boost actions"
defaults OFF. OFF uses "Shared Boost 1 settings" and "Shared Boost 2 settings". ON uses complete
settings from "Movement-start boost settings" or "Aim-start boost settings", according to the
captured starting function.

Each bank can have different enabled stages, bindings, actions, repeat timing, delays and
retention. Direct touches follow the saved starting role's bank, not the last gesture or whichever
function they later enter. A retained action keeps the bank/settings that created it. Status
labels identify these bank-specific actions.

New bank settings are detached copies of shared settings when first initialized. Later shared
edits do not keep synchronizing them. Turning separate actions OFF preserves both banks. Editing a
bank does not itself enable separate actions.

Each bank menu offers "Custom radial geometry", "Boost 1 action settings" and "Boost 2 action
settings". "Copy Aim actions here (keep enable choices)" or its Movement counterpart explicitly
replaces that destination's saved action/timing/fallback-threshold settings. Destination Boost
enabled choices and custom geometry remain. The other bank remains unchanged.

"Copy Aim geometry here" or its Movement counterpart copies distances and thicknesses but keeps
the destination custom-geometry switch and action settings. Copies affect the current draft; Save
the zone to keep them.

10. EVERY BOOST ACTION CONTROL

"Boost enabled" allows that stage's action. "Saved circular threshold distance (dp)" is its
fallback raw distance from ring center. Custom geometry/closer-spacing options may override the
active threshold without erasing this saved value. Ordinary fallback B1/B2 distances default to
120/160 dp and must stay ordered.

"Up payload", "Down payload", "Left payload" and "Right payload" select bindings by finger
direction from center. "Fallback payload (blank for none)" is used when the selected direction's
field is blank. To make only Up act, put the command in Up, leave other directions blank AND leave
Fallback blank. A blank Down with a nonblank Fallback still acts. Reversing direction does not
reuse Up for Down; an already started Tap can still complete.

"Boost action" TAP gives one finite press per entry/activation; HOLD holds; REPEAT pulses
repeatedly. "Press duration (ms)" and "Release gap (ms)" default to 35/35 and govern
finite/repeating pulses.

"Hold: latch after threshold exit" keeps an activated HOLD after returning inward/leaving its
area. "Repeat: continue after threshold exit" keeps REPEAT running after exit. These switches
apply to their respective actions and default OFF. Neither alone keeps output after lift.

"Retain activated output on lift" separately keeps an active Hold/Repeat after lift. It defaults
OFF. A timer that has not activated still cancels on lift. Choose retention deliberately;
returning inward, lifting and finishing are three different events.

"Threshold hysteresis (dp)" prevents repeated on/off activation when a finger wobbles at a drag
threshold. At 120 dp with 2 dp hysteresis, an entered stage can stay active down to about 118 dp.
Closer/custom geometry caps excessive hysteresis so a compressed band cannot become trapped
active. It does not enlarge the finite direct area or change stored ring radii.

"Direct taps enabled" permits fresh touches in that stage's finite direct band. "Direct area inner
distance (dp)" and "Direct area outer distance (dp)" store fallback band edges. Their default B1
interval is 120-144 dp; B2 is 160-184 dp. Automatic closer placement or custom geometry can
replace their effective positions/widths. Visible Aim/Movement keep fresh-touch priority. At a
shared direct-band edge, the outer B2 selection has priority.

Only B2 offers "Boost 2 activation delay (ms)", default 0. "Cancel pending Boost 2 delay on
threshold exit" defaults ON. ON cancels when you leave before activation; another entry starts a
new wait. OFF preserves the original pending timer. For HOLD to activate after you already
returned inward, also enable Hold latch. For REPEAT, also enable Repeat continuation. Keeping a
countdown is different from allowing the action outside its area. Lift before activation still
cancels it.

11. CUSTOM RADIAL GEOMETRY AND PREVIEWS

In either bank's "Custom radial geometry", "Use custom Movement boost geometry" or "Use custom Aim
boost geometry" defaults OFF and is independent of separate actions. You can use custom Aim
spacing with shared actions, or separate actions with automatic spacing.

"Distance from ring center to Boost 1 (dp)" places B1's drag threshold. "Boost 1 thickness (dp)"
sets the gap to B2. "Boost 2 thickness (dp)" supplies its displayed/direct band width. "Direct-tap
start distance from center (dp)" separately places direct B1; direct B2 follows it using B1's
thickness. Sliders/exact fields update the derived B2 and outer-edge readout.

Example: drag start 60, B1 thickness 28, B2 thickness 24 gives drag B2 at 88 and a displayed outer
edge at 112 dp. Direct start 88 gives direct B1 88-116 and B2 116-140 dp. The same widths are
used, but drag and direct starts can differ.

Custom Aim drag bands cannot start inside inner Aim. Movement drag bands cannot start inside
Movement. Direct bands must clear visible Movement, or Aim when Movement is disabled. Actual base
targets retain their priority at the exact shared edge. There is no inside-Aim Boost option.

Dragging is cumulative: reaching B2 also qualifies B1, and passing the displayed outer B2 edge
does not stop a drag stage. Direct touches are finite: direct B2 starts only B2 and leaving its
actual band applies exit rules. Thickness therefore does not turn cumulative swipes into finite
isolated buttons.

Edit draws actual-size guides behind existing controls, labeled for the saved starting bank;
dotted guides show direct areas. They follow outline dragging and rollback. "Preview boost
reachability" lets you inspect Movement or Aim and identifies the actual saved direct bank. The
shown summon uses a sample position, not your eventual Play touch. Beyond-canvas directions or
overlapping visible controls can limit access.

Boost guides remain invisible in Play. They do not enlarge the visible ring's fitting bounds. The
ring and Finish are translated to fit, never shrunk; an oversized visible ring gives a fit error.
Disabled options retain their numbers for later use.

12. FINISH, OTHER OWNERS AND SAVING REUSABLE SETTINGS

"Finish ignores swipes" defaults OFF. Normally, deliberately dragging into Finish ends the ring.
ON ignores Finish crossings during a captured drag, including the final lift; the ordinary lift
action still happens, including an eligible release-shot. A fresh separate touch on Finish still
ends it. The active-instance Finish list is available by holding Edit in Play when Finish is
covered.

Finish stops that instance's outputs/timers and hides it. The orange Re-center centers Movement
without ending the ring. Scoped Stop fire keeps independent ADS/Movement; Release ADS and End
suppression have separate purposes. Ordinary buttons can assign these through their "Floating /
Camera Follow options" with a "Scoped action" and "Action target". Release All remains global.
Another phone/face/voice source legitimately holding the same output can prevent a full release or
mask pulse edges; ring feedback identifies such conflicts.

"Save / apply named boost profiles" -> "Save as new" captures one "Save boost settings from"
source: Shared, Movement or Aim. "Profile name" names that library snapshot. Shared is available
while separate actions are OFF. "Include optional ring aiming / firing preset" additionally
captures Aim tuning and knob actions, not both banks' geometry.

"Apply boost settings only" asks for ONE destination. It preserves destination enabled choices,
custom geometry, other bank, ring dimensions/start options, Movement and Aim/firing tuning. "Apply
saved ring aiming / actions" is the separate optional operation: it preserves the Aim enabled
switch and knob identities/positions/enabled switches, mapping actions by knob slot. A mismatched
knob count is rejected atomically.

"Save full ring / activation zone preset" captures the whole current ring/zone draft, both banks
including inactive options, both saved orientation rectangles, enabled switches, tuning and start
options. "Preset name" names this full snapshot. "Add a new ring to this profile" creates a
separate independent ring to review and Save. Rename/delete changes the library, not previously
added rings. Full-profile backup includes these libraries. Live finger positions, timers and held
outputs are never saved as settings.

For save errors, check finite positive geometry, valid enabled start/destination, and whole-number
delays. "Firing delay (ms)" must be 0-60000 and at least one "Firing qualifies" stage must remain
selected even while qualified firing is OFF. At least one "Fine aim qualifies" stage must also
remain selected even while fine Aim is OFF. Disable these features with "Enable qualified boost
firing" or "Fine aim on return from selected boosts", rather than unchecking every qualifier.
Press/release durations must be whole numbers from 1-60000. Inactive settings still need valid
values.

ADS and fire need different outputs. Boost Tap/Repeat cannot share an output with a competing held
ADS/fire or another finite/repeating Boost. Do not give the same RT both a delayed B2 source and
qualified Aim firing. Two Hold owners may share a binding, but one releasing cannot release the
other's hold. Separate controls/banks can retain legitimate claims; Finish cleans only its owner.
Page/profile/orientation/connection changes and app cleanup end live sessions.

13. PRACTICAL SETTING COMBINATIONS

These are starting examples, not your installed settings. Use an available game binding, preview
reachability, and save a separate controller profile copy if you want to retain an existing setup.
Unless listed, leave optional features OFF and retention OFF. Distances below are dp, timers ms.

A. MOVEMENT THAT KEEPS GOING, WITH ONE-TAP ORANGE STOP

Set starting role MOVEMENT, Movement enabled ON, Inner aiming enabled OFF, LEFT_STICK, LINEAR,
sensitivity 1, Initial X/Y 0/0, Movement radius 88. Set Auto-center on lift OFF. Keep both Boosts
OFF.

Drag 44 dp for about half-strength movement and lift; it continues. Tap the orange Re-center to
stop Movement while leaving the ring available. Another ring's Aim/ADS/fire is not stopped by the
ring-Movement portion of recenter. Turn Auto-center on lift ON instead if every lift should stop
movement.

B. SIMPLE AIM ONLY

Set starting role AIM with the centered knob enabled, Movement enabled OFF, Inner aiming enabled
ON, Aim output MOUSE, Normal aim sensitivity 1, Mouse profile LINEAR_RELATIVE and Continue aiming
outside inner boundary ON. Set knob ADS blank and Fire mode NONE. Keep Boosts, qualified firing
and fine aiming OFF.

This is a movable aiming surface with no firing binding. Try sensitivity 0.7 if travel is too
strong. For controller Aim instead, choose RIGHT_STICK, initial-grab mapping, full displacement
220 px, deadzone 8 px and Aim auto-center on lift ON; game settings will still affect the result.

C. ADS FROM B1, DELAYED RT FROM B2, THEN RETURN TO AIM

Start AIM; enable Aiming participates in boosts and Compact boosts while aiming. Keep separate
actions OFF and use Shared settings. Leave knob ADS blank, Fire mode NONE and qualified firing OFF
so the knob supplies no competing RT.

B1: enabled, Fallback LT:1.0, directions blank, HOLD, Hold latch ON, retain on lift OFF. B2:
enabled, Fallback RT:1.0, directions blank, HOLD, activation delay 300, cancel pending B2 delay on
exit OFF, Hold latch ON, retain on lift OFF. Keep Repeat continuation OFF.

Sweep beyond B1 to hold ADS, then beyond B2 to start the 300 ms RT wait. You may return inward
before expiry: cancel OFF preserves the timer AND Hold latch ON permits activation outside B2.
Lift before activation cancels it. Lift after activation releases both holds because retention is
OFF. Optional fine Aim: enable it at 0.4 and qualify B1; returning inward applies it while ADS is
latched during that gesture.

For ADS to stay held between touches, change only B1's Retain activated output on lift to ON. B1's
Hold latch remains ON. Then LT survives lift until Release ADS, Finish or cleanup; leave B2
retention OFF so RT still ends on lift. Enable Keep fine sensitivity while qualifying ADS is
latched if that fine setting should persist with retained ADS. Orange Re-center does not release
this retained ADS.

For fire that is allowed only while you remain beyond B2, use cancel-on-exit ON and Hold latch OFF
instead. Those settings deliberately cancel a fast return before the delay finishes.

D. BOOST-ENTRY FIRE TIMER, WITHOUT A COMPETING B2 RT BINDING

Start AIM, Aim participation ON, compact Aim spacing ON. Set knob ADS LT:1.0, Fire mode HOLD, Fire
payload RT:1.0. Enable qualified boost firing, qualify B1 only, Start fire delay when entering
Boost ON, Firing delay 300, Continue fire outside aim area ON and cancel pending firing delay on
inner exit ON.

Enable B1 but leave its Fallback/directional bindings blank. Keep B2 disabled or use a
noncompeting blank binding. B1 can qualify the timer without producing another output. Crossing B1
starts the wait: return after 200 ms and about 100 ms remains. It can fire while still outside
because fire continuation is ON. Lift before activation cancels; lift after activation ends
ADS/fire with retention OFF.

To require Aim return before firing, turn Continue fire outside aim area OFF; an expired timer
waits ready instead of starting another delay. For return-timed rather than entry-timed fire, turn
Start fire delay when entering Boost OFF. Do not also add delayed RT to B2.

E. DIFFERENT MOVEMENT AND AIM BOOST ACTIONS

Enable separate Movement / Aim boost actions. Keep Movement LEFT_STICK and use mouse Aim or
RIGHT_STICK. Enable participation for both functions you want to use.

Movement bank: B1 enabled, Fallback X360A, HOLD, latch/retain OFF; B2 disabled. Aim bank: B1
enabled, Fallback LT:1.0, HOLD, Hold latch ON, retain OFF; B2 disabled. Keep knob Fire mode NONE
initially. Choose bank actions deliberately; changing tabs does not copy them.

Optional independent geometry: Movement custom ON, drag start 88, thicknesses 40/24, direct start
88. Aim custom ON, drag start 48, thicknesses 40/24, direct start 88. With default radii this
gives Movement B2 at 128 and Aim B2 at 88. Direct taps can stay OFF. Saved AIM sends later direct
taps to Aim actions; saved MOVEMENT sends them to Movement actions.

F. OPTIONAL DIRECT FINE AIMING

Set Summon starting role AIM and Inner aiming enabled ON. Use Aim output MOUSE, Mouse profile
LINEAR_RELATIVE and Normal aim sensitivity 1. Enable the fine setting at 0.4, then enable Start
aiming immediately on direct Boost touch. Select an enabled Direct boost aim destination. Keep
Continue aiming outside inner boundary ON if you want aiming immediately in the outside direct
band. Set the destination knob Fire mode NONE, ADS blank and qualified firing OFF. Immediate fine
applies even if that direct stage is not selected as a return qualifier.

Keep separate actions OFF. In Shared Boost 1 settings, set Boost enabled ON, Direct taps enabled
ON, action HOLD and all payloads blank; keep B2 disabled. For example use Aim custom geometry:
drag start 48, thicknesses 40/24, direct start 88, with Movement radius 88 and inner Aim 48. B1
direct band is 88-128; B2's reference band is 128-152 but remains disabled. You can later add a
desired B1 binding; blank gives a direct fine-Aim entry without another command.

A fresh touch on that surfaced direct band starts a neutral fine-Aim grab immediately. Saved
starting role AIM makes it use Aim bank actions. For a dormant zone to start there immediately,
choose the corresponding BOOST starting role and set its explicit bank to Aim. This still starts
only the selected Boost; B2-to-B1 return and entry-timed firing remain separate opt-ins.
